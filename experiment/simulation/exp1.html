Dynamic Synchronous Machine Simulation

Build the circuit visually, connect the phases, and watch the machine rotate with picture-based wiring and animated flow.

Procedure

  1. To make connections
    Click on radio buttons "1 & R", "2 & Y" and "3 & B" respectively
  2. Close the 3-Phase switch to supply the synchronous machine
  3. Observe clockwise rotation when phase sequence is R-Y-B
  4. Open the 3-Phase switch and close the 2-Phase DC switch
    Observe anticlockwise rotation
  5. Change phase sequence to make the machine rotate in the same direction
  6. Open the DC switch
  7. Click on "Clear Connections" button
  8. Select "1 & R", "2 & B" and "3 & Y"
  9. Close the 3-Phase switch
    The rotation becomes anticlockwise
  10. Hence phase sequence is same
